## SF-1 (security, blocking for NOC-3 and NOC-4; `docs/decisions/SF-1-l2-busy-drop.md`)

- **Finding:** the L2 boundary has one receive quarantine and drops packets while busy, so SPOOF traffic can suppress the
  RoT's GRANT and REVOKE. A suppressed REVOKE leaves a revoked tile authorized.
- **Evidence:** debug trace, GRANT busy-dropped (reason 7) at 19.01 µs.
- **Bench consequence:** the repaired `tb_pq_endpoint_sys` waits for actual GRANT delivery before S2, so **it no longer
  exercises this collision**. SF-1 needs its own attack test.
- **Required before NOC-3/NOC-4 acceptance:**
  - a design fix (options A–D in the SF-1 doc; recommendation: reserved RoT slot plus a per-source rate limit;
    Kalash chooses);
  - a SPOOF-flood-during-REVOKE attack test in which the tile ends de-authorized;
  - a single-slot drop-when-busy mutant that this test kills.
