   - **Design finding (NOC-3/NOC-4, related to C-6):** with one quarantine slot and drop-when-busy, attacker traffic can
     suppress trusted RoT GRANT and REVOKE packets. A suppressed REVOKE is a security issue. Tracked for I-5/I-6.
